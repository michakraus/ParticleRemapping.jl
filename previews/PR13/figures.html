Figures and animations ​

Every chapter of a construction shows its algorithm, its remap and its standard experiments with the same pictures, and uses one style for every figure and animation. This page states the style and lists every figure and animation of the manual and of the notes.

Style ​

remap_theme sets the fonts, Wong's colour cycle and the defaults; the documentation and the figure scripts apply it with set_theme!(remap_theme()). The plots of every construction follow these rules:

  • Markers in phase space are coloured and sized by weight: the colour map viridis, the marker area proportional to the weight, and one colour range and one size reference for all panels or frames of a figure. For 200 markers or more, the colour range and the size reference run from the 0.5 % to the 99.5 % quantile of the weights, so that a few extreme weights do not wash out the others; the colour scale is linear unless a figure asks for colorscale = log10. A figure whose point is another property of the markers, such as the slice of each marker, colours by that property and says so in its caption.

  • Target points are open circles in vermillion; displacements are thin grey segments; outlines of holes and regions are dark grey.

  • Curves and series take the colours of Wong's palette in order.

  • Fields use cividis when they are non-negative (a density, a count, a magnitude) and balance with a symmetric colour range when they are signed.

  • Categories, such as slices, cycle through Wong's palette.

  • Panels of a multi-panel figure are titled "(a) …", "(b) …". Axis labels of quantities are LaTeX strings, such as and ; labels in words are plain text.

  • Animations are .mp4 files at 20 frames per second, with the stage or the parameter in the title, and they hold the first and the last frame for a moment.

Inventory ​

The kinds of pictures: the standard pictures S1, S2, A1, T1, G1, G2 and X1 above; C, a step of the construction; W, a worked example in closed form; E, a numerical experiment; N, a solver and its convergence; L, a long run with repeated remaps; D, a demonstration of a structural fact. A figure of a note is labelled by its \label; an animation is named in the note by its file. The figure scripts scripts/<construction>/make_figures.jl write every file to results/<construction>/, from where the notes include them.

Coverage. Every construction has G1 and G2 on its overview page, S1, S2, A1 and T1 on the example of its overview page, and X1 on its page Numerical experiments; each note shows S1 and S2. The kinds C, E, N and D differ between the constructions, because each shows the steps and facts of its own construction. The long run with repeated remaps (L) of the standard experiments runs for the gauge rezoning, the canonical transport and the sliced redistribution; the projection fails at its second or third remap, and the spline-projection redistribution does not reach the marker counts of the run with its defaults (K2).

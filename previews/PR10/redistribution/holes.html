Hole filling ​

This page runs SplineRedistribution on a marker set with a hole, outside the verification set-up. Here the symplectic step misses its targets (known issues K3). The script scripts/redistribution/failure_cases.jl prints every number of this page.

A hole: the step misses its targets (K3) ​

The 244 markers of hole_filling_problem with nL = 16, hole_a = 0.9 and hole_b = 0.8 (a   lattice on    with an elliptic hole), redistributed by SplineRedistribution(; Vmax = 4.0, relax_step = 0.006 * 16 / 256), the relaxation step scaled down with the number of pairs. The remap takes 44 s and returns without an error:

The contraction number   puts far outside the regime of the parameter solver, and the forward Newton solve does not converge: the end point is not a step of the scheme, so neither the targets nor the total momentum are reached, and only the KKT weights keep the total weight. remap does not report the failure; only info.target_error and info.residual show it (K3).

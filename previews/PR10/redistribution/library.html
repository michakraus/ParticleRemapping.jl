Library: Redistribution ​

ParticleRemapping.Redistribution Module
julia
Redistribution

Symplectic redistribution in phase space: a positive, moment-compatible B-spline projection and translation- and permutation-invariant pairwise spline Hamiltonians whose coefficients solve a linear inverse problem.

The method ​

ParticleRemapping.Redistribution.SplineRedistribution Type
julia
SplineRedistribution(; Lx = 2π, Vmax = 3.5, nx = 8, nv = 10, degree = 3, c_min = 1e-4,
    ε_n = 1e-6, σx = π/2, σv = 1.2, relax_step = 0.006, relax_iterations = 10, v_wall = 2,
    k_wall = 2.5, nx_cells = 10, np_cells = 8, p_scale_factor = 2.5,
    scheme = ImplicitMidpointStep(), substeps = 1, τ = 1)

The symplectic redistribution of 1D-1V markers by a positive, moment-compatible spline projection and a pairwise spline Hamiltonian:

  1. project the markers onto the PhaseSpaceSplines (nx × nv cells of degree degree) by the positive lumped projection (c_min);

  2. relax the points to targets (x̄, v̄) (declustering_relaxation with σx, σv, relax_step, relax_iterations, v_wall, k_wall);

  3. weigh the targets by the kkt_redistribution_weights with the number_density_volumes (ε_n), so that p̄ = m w̄ v̄ conserves the discrete mass and momentum;

  4. solve for the parameters of a PairwiseSplineHamiltonian (nx_cells, np_cells, p_s = p_scale_factor max |p|, |p̄|) and advance by the symplectic scheme in substeps steps of artificial step τ (redistribution_substeps);

  5. assign the weights w̄ and the velocities v = p / (m w̄) to the end point.

The states of the redistribution of before that gave result: the markers before; the same markers, the stage at which the relaxed targets (x̄, v̄) and their KKT weights are computed; the end point of each symplectic step (one per substep) at the old weights, v = p / (m w); and the markers of result, after the gauge step that assigns the KKT weights w̄ at fixed (x, p). The 2 × |I| discrete moment matrix A = [ΔΩᵀ E; m (ΔΩ ∘ v̄)ᵀ E] of the mass and momentum of the weights w̄_a = ΔΩ_a f_h(x̄_a, v̄_a) (1D-1V).

ParticleRemapping.Redistribution.kkt_spline_projection Function
julia
kkt_spline_projection(c_pos, A, moments)

The weighted KKT projection c^KKT = c^pos − W⁻¹ Aᵀ (A W⁻¹ Aᵀ)⁻¹ (A c^pos − moments) with W = diag(c^pos)⁻²: the minimiser of ½ (c − c^pos)ᵀ W (c − c^pos) subject to A c = moments. With the kkt_moment_matrix A and moments = [Σ w_a, Σ p_a] the weights ΔΩ̄_a f_h^KKT(x̄_a, v̄_a) conserve the discrete mass and momentum.

Ensemble Hamiltonians ​

ParticleRemapping.Redistribution.PairwiseSplineHamiltonian Type
julia
PairwiseSplineHamiltonian(Lx, p_scale; nx_cells = 8, np_cells = 6, degree = 4)

The pairwise ensemble Hamiltonian

julia
H_θ(q, p) = p_s Σ_a Σ_r θ⁰_r χ_r(p_a/p_s)
          + p_s/(2N) Σ_{a,b} Σ_{m,l,r} θ²_{m,l,r} φ_m(q_a − q_b) χ_l(p_a/p_s) χ_r(p_b/p_s)

with periodic B-splines φ_m of degree degree on [0, Lx) (nx_cells cells, so M_x = nx_cells) and clamped B-splines χ_r of the same degree on [−1, 1] (np_cells cells, M_p = np_cells + degree), in the scaled momentum p/p_s. The parameter vector holds θ⁰ followed by θ² in the column-major order of (m, l, r); its length is M_p + M_x M_p² (hamiltonian_parameter_count).

ParticleRemapping.Redistribution.FourierMomentHamiltonian Type
julia
FourierMomentHamiltonian(wavenumbers, p_scale; np_cells = 6, degree = 4)

The pairwise ensemble Hamiltonian of PairwiseSplineHamiltonian with the Fourier basis φ = {cos(k Δq), sin(k Δq)}_{k ∈ K} on the torus in place of the periodic splines. Its double sum factors through the collective moments (collective_moments), so that ensemble_hamiltonian and ensemble_hamiltonian_gradient cost O(N |K| M_p²). The parameter vector holds θ⁰ (length M_p), then the cosine coefficients θᶜ_{k,l,r} and the sine coefficients θˢ_{k,l,r}, each |K| × M_p × M_p in column-major order.

ParticleRemapping.Redistribution.CentroidFrameHamiltonian Type
julia
CentroidFrameHamiltonian(Qmax, p_scale; nq_cells = 8, np_cells = 6, degree = 4)

The centroid-frame ensemble Hamiltonian on ℝ, H_θ(q, p) = Σ_a h_θ(q_a − q̄(q), p_a) with the centroid q̄ = N⁻¹ Σ_b q_b and h_θ(q̃, p) = p_s Σ_{i,r} θ_{i,r} B_i(q̃) χ_r(p/p_s), clamped B-splines B_i on [−Qmax, Qmax] (nq_cells cells) and χ_r on [−1, 1] (np_cells cells). The parameters θ_{i,r} are in column-major order.

ParticleRemapping.Redistribution.forward_redistribution_step Function
julia
forward_redistribution_step(H, scheme, q, p, θ; τ = 1, tol = 1e-14, maxiter = 30,

One step of scheme with the fixed parameters θ from (q, p), solved by Newton's method with a homotopy θ → s θ over the values s of homotopy (the default is (0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1) for the midpoint and (0.2, 0.4, …, 1) for symplectic Euler), which follows the identity-connected branch. ParticleRemapping.Redistribution.redistribution_substeps Function
julia

The composition of substeps redistribution steps towards the targets z_k = z + (k/K)(z̄ − z): each solves solve_redistribution_parameters from the end point of the previous step and advances by forward_redistribution_step.

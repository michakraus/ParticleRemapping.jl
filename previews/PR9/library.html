Common library ​

ParticleRemapping.ParticleRemapping Module
julia
ParticleRemapping

Symplectic remapping, projection and redistribution of weighted particle-in-cell markers.

A marker carries a position x, a velocity v and a weight w; its canonical momentum is p = m w v. The package collects the constructions of four notes on maps that move, reweight or redistribute such markers while they keep the canonical form Σ dx ∧ dp:

  • Remapping: the factorisation of every symplectic redistribution into a canonical map and a gauge step, velocity rezoning by the gauge step under charge and energy constraints, and canonical transport with current-quantile matching (1D-1V).

  • Projection: kick–drift and non-separable remap Hamiltonians with a centroid shift that conserve the total momentum, fitted to target points from a projected density; charge-conserving reweighting and target weights from a dynamical low-rank density.

  • Redistribution: a positive, moment-compatible B-spline projection and translation- and permutation-invariant pairwise spline Hamiltonians, solved for their coefficients through a linear inverse problem.

  • SlicedRedistribution: sliced quantile optimal-transport cotangent lifts and the canonical weight squeeze.

The common layer — Markers, the canonical structure, grids and bases, the remap interface — is shared by all four. Plotting lives in the Makie extension.

The common layer holds what the four constructions share: the marker sets, the canonical structure, the grids and bases, the remap interface and the plots of marker sets. The names of each construction are on its own library page; using ParticleRemapping makes all of them available.

Markers ​

Markers(x, v, w; m = 1)

An ensemble of N weighted markers of mass m.

In one dimension x and v are vectors of length N; in d dimensions they are d × N matrices with one column per marker. The weights w are a vector of length N. The canonical momentum of marker a is p_a = m w_a v_a (momentum).

ParticleRemapping.symplecticity_defect Function
julia
symplecticity_defect(R, z; jacobian = ForwardDiff.jacobian) -> (defect, ‖DR‖)

The largest entry of DRᵀ J DR − J, with DR the Jacobian of the map R at the packed state z, and the operator norm of DR. The defect is zero (to round-off) for a symplectic map. Pass jacobian = fd_jacobian for a map that does not accept dual numbers.

ParticleRemapping.presymplectic_matrix Function
julia
presymplectic_matrix(z; m = 1)

The matrix of the presymplectic form ω_N = Σ_a m (w_a dx_a ∧ dv_a + v_a dx_a ∧ dw_a) at the state z = (x_1, v_1, w_1, x_2, v_2, w_2, …) of 1D-1V markers. It is the pull-back of Σ dx_a ∧ dp_a by (x, v, w) ↦ (x, p = m w v) and is degenerate: its kernel is spanned by w_a ∂_{w_a} − v_a ∂_{v_a}.

ParticleRemapping.pullback_residual Function
julia
pullback_residual(Φ, z; m = 1, jacobian = fd_jacobian)

The largest entry of DΦᵀ Ω(Φ(z)) DΦ − Ω(z) with Ω the presymplectic_matrix: zero when the map Φ of 1D-1V marker states (x, v, w) preserves the presymplectic form ω_N.

Grids and bases ​

ParticleRemapping.PeriodicGrid Type
julia
PeriodicGrid(L, Ng)

A periodic grid on [0, L) with Ng nodes x_i = (i - 1) Δx, Δx = L / Ng, and the linear B-spline (hat) shape functions S_i. On the cell [x_i, x_{i+1}) the only non-zero shape functions are S_i(x) = 1 − (x − x_i)/Δx and S_{i+1}(x) = (x − x_i)/Δx, with i + 1 taken modulo Ng; they form a partition of unity.

ParticleRemapping.shape_matrix Function
julia

The N × Ng matrix with entries S_i(x_a).

ParticleRemapping.grid_moment Function

The grid moments M_i^(k) = Σ_a w_a S_i(x_a) v_a^k, i = 1, …, Ng, of 1D-1V markers. The grid charge is q M^(0), the grid current q M^(1), and (m/2) M^(2) the kinetic energy that each shape function collects.

The states of a remap ​

Every construction gives the states of its remap, from the markers before it to the markers after it, as a vector of RemapStage: remap_states for a figure with one panel per stage, and remap_frames for an animation.

ParticleRemapping.RemapStage Type
julia
RemapStage(markers, label)

One state of a remap: the markers after a stage of the map, with a label for a figure panel or an animation frame. Every construction returns the states of its remap as a vector of stages, from the markers before the remap to the markers after it, for plot_stages and animate_stages.

ParticleRemapping.remap_states Function
julia
remap_states(method::AbstractRemap, before::Markers, result::RemapResult) -> Vector{RemapStage}

The states of the remap of before by method that gave result: the markers before the remap, after each stage of the canonical map ψ (at the old weights, v = p / (m w)), and after the gauge step that sets the new weights, which are the markers of result. Each construction adds a method for its remap types. plot_stages draws the states.

ParticleRemapping.remap_frames Function
julia
remap_frames(method::AbstractRemap, before::Markers, result::RemapResult; kwargs...)
    -> Vector{RemapStage}

The frames of an animation of the remap of before by method that gave result, for animate_stages. The generic method interpolates the remap_states with interpolate_stages and passes kwargs to it; a construction whose map has a natural path, such as a homotopy or the flow of a Hamiltonian, adds a method that follows that path.

julia
remap_frames(method::Union{KickDriftRemap, NonSeparableRemap}, before, result;
    frames_per_stage = 30, hold = 8)

The frames of an animation of the remap along its natural path: the parameters of each kick and each drift, or of each non-separable stage, scaled from zero to their fitted values in frames_per_stage frames, one sub-step after the other, at the old weights; then the gauge steps of remap_states at fixed (x, p), interpolated by interpolate_stages. Each state is held for hold frames. A sub-step with its parameters at zero is the identity, so the path passes through every state of remap_states.

julia
remap_frames(method::GaugeRezoning, before::Markers, result::RemapResult;
    frames_per_step = 6, hold = 8) -> Vector{RemapStage}

The frames of an animation of the gauge rezoning that follow its homotopy: hold copies of the markers before the remap, then frames_per_step frames between each pair of consecutive accepted points of the homotopy in result.info.history, with the log-stretches μ interpolated linearly (each frame is a gauge step of before), and hold copies of the result.

julia
remap_frames(method::CurrentQuantileMatching, before::Markers, result::RemapResult;
    period = (0, L), kwargs...) -> Vector{RemapStage}

The frames of an animation of the canonical transport: its states (remap_states) interpolated by interpolate_stages, with the positions periodic on the domain [0, L) of the grid; kwargs go to interpolate_stages.

julia
remap_frames(method::SplineRedistribution, before::Markers, result::RemapResult;
    s = range(0, 1; length = 41), hold = 8, frames_per_stage = 30) -> Vector{RemapStage}

The frames of an animation of the redistribution of before that gave result. The markers hold before and at the stage of the relaxed targets; each symplectic step then follows its pseudo-time path Ψ_{s τ}^θ (pseudotime_path) for the values s (from 0 to 1) at the old weights; the gauge step to the KKT weights is interpolated at fixed (x, p) (interpolate_stages, frames_per_stage). Every stage holds for hold frames.

julia
remap_frames(method::SlicedQuantileRemap, before::Markers, result::RemapResult;
             frames_per_stage = 30, hold = 8) -> Vector{RemapStage}

The frames of an animation of the sliced remap of before that gave result, along a path of canonical maps: frames_per_stage frames of Ψ_q and of Ψ_p with the relaxation parameters β_mean, β_intra of the frozen plan scaled by s ∈ (0, 1] (the identity at s = 0); of the squeeze T_μ with the multipliers (1 − s) + s μ, which keep Σ μ = N; all at the old weights, v = p / (m w). Then the gauge step at the fixed state (x̄, p̄) with the weights (1 − s) w + s w̄. Each state of remap_states is held for hold frames, and the label of a frame is that of the state it moves towards. The positions are mapped into [−Lx, Lx).

ParticleRemapping.interpolate_stages Function
julia
interpolate_stages(stages; frames_per_stage = 30, hold = 8, period = nothing)

The frames of an animation through stages: frames_per_stage frames between each pair of consecutive stages and hold copies of every stage. Between two stages the positions, the momenta p = m w v and the weights change linearly, and the velocities are v = p / (m w). A canonical stage, at fixed weights, thus moves the markers on straight lines, and a gauge step, at fixed (x, p), moves them along v ∝ 1/w. With period = (a, b) the positions are periodic on [a, b) and move by the shorter way round. The label of each frame is that of the stage it moves towards.

Standard experiments ​

The standard experiments, which every construction runs.

ParticleRemapping.STANDARD_METHODS Constant
julia
STANDARD_METHODS

The keys of the constructions that run the standard experiments, in the order of the manual: :projection (kick–drift map), :nonseparable (non-separable stages), :spline (spline-projection redistribution), :gauge (gauge rezoning), :transport (canonical transport with the identity map) and :sliced (sliced redistribution).

ParticleRemapping.standard_method Function
julia
standard_method(key, experiment; N = nothing, density = nothing)

The method of the construction key (one of STANDARD_METHODS) for the standard experiment experiment, :checkerboard or :wave, with the parameters of the manual. N is the number of markers of a reduced run, for which the spline-projection redistribution scales its relaxation step to 0.006 · 16 / N; nothing, the full size of the standard experiments, keeps its defaults. density(x, v) is the density on the natural box of the experiment that the constructions with a target density receive, the projection and the transport; the other constructions ignore it. Without it, the projection projects the density of the markers and the transport takes the represented density of the experiment.

julia

The remap method of a construction on [0, L), applied to markers on [−L/2, L/2): the positions are shifted by shift into [0, L) before the remap and back into [−L/2, L/2) after it. The velocities and the weights are those of method. remap returns the info of method.

ParticleRemapping.checkerboard_density Function
julia
checkerboard_density(x, v; variant = :represented)

A density of the checkerboard, with total weight 1 on [−1, 1]² and zero outside. With variant = :represented, the density that the checkerboard_markers represent: 1/2 in the quadrants Q1 = [0, 1)² and Q3 = [−1, 0)², zero in Q2 and Q4. With variant = :positive, 1/4 on [−1, 1]²: the markers are a sample of it with a hole, since their weights 1/N are those of a sample of 1/4 with the uniform marker density N/4 on [−1, 1]², and Q2 and Q4 hold no marker.

ParticleRemapping.electric_wave_density Function
julia
electric_wave_density(x, v; variant = :represented, steps = 500, Δt = 0.05, m = 1.2,

Verification: sliced redistribution ​

Every number of the sliced redistribution comes from a test in test/sliced/, a script in scripts/sliced/ or an @example block of its pages. The functions of SlicedRedistribution port the source verification script of the construction, verify_symplectic_redistribution.jl of the sliced construction in the particle-in-cell project; the package replaces it there, and the project's note Sliced-Symplectic-Particle-Redistribution.tex is written from this chapter. The derivation that produced the script is the source of the observations marked derivation on the page Constructions that fail. The tables of the suites are on the structural suites, hole filling and long-time run pages.

Comparison with the source script ​

test/sliced/forwarddiff.jl holds, in a module ScriptReference, the source script's code verbatim: its hand-written forward-mode Dual number, its linear solve, its Jacobian jacobian_dual, its canonical matrix, its sliced lift and its weight squeeze. At the plan and the multipliers of suite 3 (16 markers) and a random state it compares

  • the values of the port (both lifts and the squeeze) with those of the script, and the lift with and without anchors, to the relative tolerance ;

  • the ForwardDiff Jacobians of the port with the script's Dual Jacobians, of the composed map and of the first lift, to . The two differ by the pivoting of the linear solves only.

The thresholds of test/sliced/experiments.jl, test/sliced/experiments_long_time.jl and scripts/sliced/verify_sliced_redistribution.jl are those of the source script. The reference numbers in the two test files come from a cold run (Julia 1.13.4, aarch64) of the working copy of the source script, with its own Dual numbers; the copy in the project does not parse (see the findings). The defects are round-off and differ from those of the script in the last digits; the controls agree with the script to the round-off of the Jacobians ( ), so the tests require a relative agreement of .

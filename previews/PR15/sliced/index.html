Sliced redistribution ​

The sliced redistribution moves clustered markers back towards an even cover of a box     without a solve for parameters. Its canonical map of is an explicit composition of two sliced quantile cotangent lifts and a linear canonical weight squeeze; the gauge step that follows sets the weights  , with   and the multipliers of the squeeze. It answers the obstruction of uncoupled maps by slices: each lift couples the markers of a slice, and within a slice of small velocity range it spreads the positions over the whole interval, whatever their correlation with the velocity. A lift of the marginals alone cannot fill a hole whose marginals are uniform; the slices can (hole filling on a checkerboard). For frozen slices, anchors and multipliers the map satisfies  ; it keeps the total momentum and the total weight to round-off. The multipliers of the remap are computed from the markers, so the map is symplectic for their frozen values (parameters that depend on the state).

The setting is that of the foundations: the canonical coordinates with  , the presymplectic form , the factorisation lemma and the symplecticity condition. This chapter marks the image of each stage by its own decoration: after the first lift, after the second, after the squeeze and for the output markers. and are the numbers of velocity and position slices,   and   their sizes. In the code the fields q̃, p̃, q̂, p̂, q̄ and p̄ of the result hold these values, and the field q_anc holds the position anchors .

The algorithm step by step ​

The map is a composition of four stages,

The figures follow the stages on 40 markers after the long run of the standard experiments, with 4 velocity slices and 8 position slices (algorithm_svg).

  1. Velocity slices. Sort the markers into slices of equal size by velocity (panel 1, the colour is the slice, the dashed lines separate the slices, the open circles are the equispaced position targets of each slice).

  2. . In each slice, move the positions towards their targets: the cotangent lift of the gradient of a strictly convex potential, which changes the momenta as well (panel 2).

  3. Position slices. Sort the markers into slices of equal size by position (panel 3).

  4. . In each slice, move the momenta towards equispaced targets, with the roles of and exchanged (panel 4).

  5. . Scale each momentum by a slot multiplier and each position offset by , a linear canonical map that keeps the total momentum; the multipliers follow the density of the input markers (panel 5, colour and size are the weight again).

  6. . Set the weights   and the velocities  , a gauge step (panel 6).

julia
using ParticleRemapping
steps = algorithm_svg(:sliced)

The player runs through the steps automatically; pause it or step back and forward through them:

julia
steps.panels

Stages 1–3 of the map are canonical maps of ; the last stage is a gauge step, which any choice of weights keeps symplectic. The order of the two lifts is that of a Knothe–Rosenblatt rearrangement (Villani, 2009): first the positions conditional on the velocity, then the momenta conditional on the position. The algorithm page gives each stage in full.

The code ​

The module ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution implements the construction for 1D-1V markers and, for   markers, along each axis (per-axis version). The method type SlicedQuantileRemap implements remap, remap_states and remap_frames; its keywords set the box, the numbers of slices, the relaxation parameters and mollifier widths of the two lifts, and the kernel widths of the multipliers. The building blocks are public: the slices and targets (sorted_slices, uniform_quantile_targets), the lifts (split_quantile_ot_lift, dual_quantile_ot_lift, per_axis_sliced_lift), the frozen plan (sliced_lift_plan, apply_sliced_lift), the squeeze and its multipliers (canonical_weight_squeeze, squeeze_multipliers) and the diagnostics (phase_cell_statistics).

The script scripts/sliced/verify_sliced_redistribution.jl runs the verification suites and prints their numbers; scripts/sliced/long_time_experiment.jl runs the variants and the remaps during the long-time run; scripts/sliced/make_figures.jl writes the figures and the animations to results/sliced/. The verification lists every test file and script of the chapter.

Standard experiments ​

Every construction of the manual remaps the same two marker sets and reports the same measures. The page Numerical experiments of each chapter shows the results of its construction; the comparison sets them side by side. The two marker sets come from the sliced chapter, where they are suites 5a and 5b: a checkerboard with two empty quadrants, and the markers of a long run in a two-wave electric field. They show the behaviour that the obstructions predict: a map that is the same for every marker keeps the area of a hole, a construction that keeps the positions cannot fill a hole in , and a construction that couples the markers can.

The standard test set ​

The standard test set grades the two experiments above into ladders of cases, so that every construction meets a weak and a strong form of each difficulty with the same measures. Each case is a StandardCase: a marker set on one box     , periodic in , with the exact density that goes with it. Where the markers represent a density with holes, the case holds both variants of case_density, :represented and :positive, as the two experiments above do; otherwise it holds one density in both.

The box. Every marker of a case lies in its box: StandardCase refuses a marker with  . Each case has one box for all its markers. For the families W, S1 and S4 the box is a keyword, which a benchmark sweeps; the families C, E, S2 and S3 keep the box of their density. A marker outside the box after a remap is a structure failure: the hat projection clamps it onto the edge nodes of the box (_hat_weights in src/projection/targets.jl), so the next deposit, remap or push sees it on the edge of the box.

The reference. GridResample puts the   markers on the cell centres of an   lattice of the box and weights them by a density: the lumped projection of the markers on the hats of the box, or the exact density of the case (REFERENCE_METHODS). It keeps the total weight and nothing else; it is not symplectic. It shows what a resample that ignores the structure gives on each case.

The measures compare the markers with the box and the density of the case: case_evenness, the cell counts on the box and on the support of the represented density (and on the window   of the wave experiment for W); case_outside, the markers outside the box and the non-finite ones; case_representation, the smoothed markers against the smoothed density; case_charge_noise, the grid charge against the exact one; case_weights, the spread of the weights; and, for a remap, case_conservation and case_displacement. case_reference computes the integrals of the density once for both measures of a case.

The driver. case_method gives the method of a construction for a case: the parameters of standard_method with the box of the case, and for the sliced map the slice counts nearest to those of the experiment scaled with among the divisors of . run_case runs a method on a case and returns one row of every measure before and after, the conservation, the displacement and the state of the solver. A DomainError, an ArgumentError or a failure of a factorisation in the remap is recorded with its class and message, any other exception is thrown again, and a non-finite result is a failure; the row records the markers outside the box after a remap and classifies nothing more.

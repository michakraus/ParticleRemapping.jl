Combining the two constructions ​

The gauge rezoning and the canonical transport differ in what they move and in what they keep exactly; the properties of the chapter list both side by side, and the comparison of all constructions sets them beside the other chapters.

The two constructions combine. The current-quantile matching gives velocities that are consistent with an estimate of ; used as targets for the gauge rezoning, they give a remap whose grid charge and kinetic energy are exact. Its grid current is exact only if preserves the grid current, the discrete form of the compatibility condition.

remap(::GaugeRezoning, markers; targets) takes such targets as log-stretches  . Here the matched velocities of   serve as targets for the charge- and energy-conserving rezoning; the rezoning keeps the momenta, so the matched velocities with the sign of are admissible targets:

julia
using ParticleRemapping
grid = PeriodicGrid(2π, 8)
target = SeparableMaxwellianTarget(grid, 0.4)
mk = quantile_matching_setup(; N = 8000, Ng = 8).markers
matched = remap(CurrentQuantileMatching(grid, (x, p) -> (x, p), target), mk).markers
μstar = log.(matched.v ./ mk.v)
r = remap(GaugeRezoning(grid; K = (0, 2), dτ0 = 0.25), mk; targets = μstar)
(τ = r.info.τ, report = conservation_report(mk, r.markers; grid))
(τ = 1.0, report = (weight = 2.6645352591003757e-15, momentum = 6.938893903907228e-18, kinetic_energy = -4.440892098500626e-16, charge = 2.6263487129620926e-15, current = 1.0857484137125312e-17, energy = 3.1088193712847416e-15))

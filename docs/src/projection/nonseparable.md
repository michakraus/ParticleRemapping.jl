# [Non-separable stages](@id projection-nonseparable)

```@meta
CurrentModule = ParticleRemapping.Projection
```

The [stage Jacobians](@ref projection-stage-jacobians) couple the compression of a kick at ``+v``
to an expansion at ``-v``. A stage that is generated by one Hamiltonian in ``x`` and ``p``
together does not have this coupling. For each stage ``s``, let ``\psi_s(\xi, v, w)`` be periodic
in ``\xi \in \mathbb{T}^d``, and let

```math
H^N_s(x, p) = \sum_a \psi_s\big(x_a - X_{\mathrm{c}}(x), \, p_a / (m w_a), \, w_a\big) , \qquad
\phi_s(\xi, v, w) = \frac{\psi_s(\xi, v, w)}{m w} .
```

The flow of ``H^N_s`` is not explicit. The stage ``N_s`` is one step of the implicit midpoint
rule,

```math
z' = z + \mathbb{J} \nabla H^N_s\big( \tfrac{1}{2} (z + z') \big) ,
```

and the artificial time step is absorbed into ``\psi_s``. The [parametrisation](@ref
projection-parametrisation) is a tensor product in all three variables:

```math
\psi_s(\xi, v, w) = w \sum_{i, j, l} \lambda^s_{ijl} \, B_i(\xi) \, \hat{B}_j(v) \, C_l(\ln w) .
```

In the velocity form below, ``\phi_s`` is then independent of the size of the weight except
through ``C_l(\ln w)``, as for the kicks and drifts.

Write ``\{A, B\}_v = \sum_i ( \partial_{x^i} A \, \partial_{v^i} B - \partial_{v^i} A \,
\partial_{x^i} B )`` for the bracket in ``(x,v)``.

!!! info "Proposition (non-separable stages)"
    Let ``\theta`` be fixed, and let the midpoint equation have a unique solution near ``z``.

    1. ``N_s`` is symplectic on ``(\mathcal{Z}, \omega)``.
    2. ``N_s`` conserves the total momentum ``P`` exactly.
    3. With ``\xi_a = x_a - X_{\mathrm{c}}``, ``v_a = p_a / (m w_a)`` and the midpoints
       ``\bar{\xi}_a = (\xi_a + \xi'_a)/2``, ``\bar{v}_a = (v_a + v'_a)/2``, the stage reads

       ```math
       \xi'_a = \xi_a + \nabla_v \phi_s(\bar{\xi}_a, \bar{v}_a, w_a) - c , \qquad
       v'_a = v_a - \nabla_\xi \phi_s(\bar{\xi}_a, \bar{v}_a, w_a) + b ,
       ```

       with ``c = \sum_j \alpha_j \nabla_v \phi_s(\bar{\xi}_j, \bar{v}_j, w_j)``,
       ``b = \sum_j \alpha_j \nabla_\xi \phi_s(\bar{\xi}_j, \bar{v}_j, w_j)`` and
       ``X_{\mathrm{c}}' = X_{\mathrm{c}} + c``. Each marker takes one implicit-midpoint step of
       ``\phi_s(\cdot, \cdot, w_a)`` in ``(\xi, v)``, and all markers share the shift ``c`` and
       the boost ``b``.
    4. Let the weights be the values of a smooth field, ``w_a = W(x_a, v_a)``, and let
       ``X_{\mathrm{c}}`` and ``b`` be held fixed. The flow of ``H^N_s`` moves the markers in
       ``y = (x, v)`` with a velocity field ``u`` of divergence

       ```math
       \operatorname{div} u = \{ W, \partial_w \phi_s \}_v ,
       ```

       evaluated at ``w = W(y)``, and the midpoint step acts on ``y`` with the Jacobian
       determinant

       ```math
       \det D N_s = 1 + \nabla W(y)^\top \big( I + \tfrac{1}{2} \mathbb{J} K \big)^{-1} \mathbb{J} \, \nabla \partial_w \phi_s ,
       ```

       where ``K = \nabla^2 \phi_s`` and ``\nabla \partial_w \phi_s`` are taken in ``(\xi, v)``
       at ``(\bar{\xi}, \bar{v}, W(y))``.

!!! details "Proof"
    (1) The implicit midpoint rule is symplectic for every Hamiltonian. (2) Under
    ``x_a \mapsto x_a + \delta`` for all ``a``, the centroid moves to ``X_{\mathrm{c}} + \delta``,
    so ``H^N_s`` is invariant and ``\sum_a \nabla_{x_a} H^N_s = 0`` at every point, in particular at
    the midpoint. By the midpoint equation, ``\sum_a p'_a = \sum_a p_a``. (3) As for the kick,
    ``\nabla_{p_a} H^N_s = \nabla_v \psi_s / (m w_a) = \nabla_v \phi_s`` and
    ``\nabla_{x_a} H^N_s = m w_a \nabla_\xi \phi_s(\xi_a, v_a, w_a) - \alpha_a \sum_j m w_j \nabla_\xi
    \phi_s(\xi_j, v_j, w_j)``, both at the midpoints. Division of the momentum equation by
    ``m w_a`` and ``\alpha_a / w_a = 1 / w_{\mathrm{tot}}`` give ``v'_a``. The positions move by
    ``x'_a = x_a + \nabla_v \phi_s``, so ``X_{\mathrm{c}}' = X_{\mathrm{c}} + c`` and
    ``\xi'_a = x'_a - X_{\mathrm{c}}'``. (4) With ``X_{\mathrm{c}}`` and ``b`` fixed, a marker moves
    with ``u(y) = \mathbb{J} \nabla \phi_s(y - (X_{\mathrm{c}}, 0), W(y)) + (0, b)``. As for the
    [compressibility bracket](@ref weight-dependent-hamiltonians),
    ``\operatorname{div} u = \operatorname{tr}(\mathbb{J} K) + \nabla W^\top \mathbb{J} \nabla
    \partial_w \phi_s``, and ``\operatorname{tr}(\mathbb{J} K) = 0`` for symmetric ``K``. The
    second term is ``\{W, \partial_w \phi_s\}_v``. For the step, differentiation of
    ``y' = y + \mathbb{J} \nabla \phi_s(\bar{y}, W(y))`` gives

    ```math
    (I - \tfrac{1}{2} \mathbb{J} K) \, \mathrm{d}y' = (I + \tfrac{1}{2} \mathbb{J} K + \mathbb{J} g \, \nabla W^\top) \, \mathrm{d}y
    ```

    with ``g = \nabla \partial_w \phi_s``. The matrix ``\mathbb{J} K`` is Hamiltonian, so its
    eigenvalues come in pairs ``\pm \lambda`` and
    ``\det(I - \tfrac{1}{2} \mathbb{J} K) = \det(I + \tfrac{1}{2} \mathbb{J} K)``. The matrix
    determinant lemma gives the determinant.

To first order in ``\phi_s``, the determinant is ``1 + \{W, \partial_w \phi_s\}_v``. For a
separable ``\phi_s = \tilde{\kappa}(\xi, w) + \tilde{\eta}(v, w)`` the bracket is
``\partial_x W \, \partial_v \partial_w \tilde{\eta} - \partial_v W \, \partial_\xi \partial_w
\tilde{\kappa}``, which is the first order of the [stage Jacobians](@ref
projection-stage-jacobians). At a point where ``\partial_x W = 0`` the bracket is
``-\partial_v W \, \partial_\xi \partial_w \phi_s(\xi, v, W)`` in every case. For a separable
``\phi_s`` the factor ``\partial_\xi \partial_w \tilde{\kappa}`` is the same at ``\pm v`` when
``W`` is even in ``v``, so the compression at ``+v`` is an expansion at ``-v``. For a
non-separable ``\phi_s`` the factor depends on ``v``. The function
``\phi_s = a \, v \sin \xi \ln w``, for example, gives
``\{W, \partial_w \phi_s\}_v = -a \, v \cos x \, \partial_v \ln W`` where ``\partial_x W = 0``.
For ``W`` proportional to a Maxwellian, at ``x = \pi``, this is ``-a v^2``: a compression at
``+v`` and at ``-v``.

``\det DN - 1`` of one marker step at ``x = \pi`` ([`marker_midpoint_step`](@ref)) for the
weight field ``W = (1 + 0.3 \cos x) e^{-v^2/2}``, the separable
``\phi = 0.1 \sin\xi \ln w + 0.05 v^2 \ln w`` and the non-separable
``\phi = 0.1 v \sin\xi \ln w`` ([`pm_v_coupling`](@ref)). At ``v = \pm 1.2`` the separable
``\phi`` gives ``\det DN - 1 = \mp 0.120``, the non-separable one ``-0.144`` at both
(`test/projection/stages.jl`).

The price is the implicit step. The fixed-point iteration of the velocity form costs one
evaluation of ``\nabla \phi_s`` per marker and iteration; the markers couple only through the
``2d`` numbers ``c`` and ``b``. The parameter vector has ``n_\xi n_v n_\ell`` entries per stage
instead of ``(n_\xi + n_v) n_\ell``. For the fit, the derivatives of the image points with
respect to ``\theta`` follow from the linearised step equations: one ``2d \times 2d`` system per
marker and one ``2d \times 2d`` system for ``c`` and ``b``, so each stage costs ``O(N)``
operations per parameter direction, as for the explicit stages.

## The code

[`nonseparable_stage`](@ref) solves one stage in the velocity form by fixed-point iteration; the
iteration stops when the update, the partials of dual numbers included, falls below ``10^{-13}``
relative to the iterate, so forward-mode derivatives through the stage converge too.
[`NonSeparableParametrisation`](@ref) holds the bases: 16 periodic splines in ``\xi``, 17 in
``v`` and 6 in ``\ln w``, two stages, 3264 parameters. [`nonseparable_map`](@ref) applies the
stages and, on request, the sensitivities from the linearised step equations. For ``d = 1`` they
read, per marker, with ``H_a = \nabla^2 \phi_s`` at the midpoint and
``S = \operatorname{diag}(1, -1)``,

```math
\begin{pmatrix} \mathrm{d}\xi'_a \\ \mathrm{d}v'_a \end{pmatrix}
= (I - \tfrac12 S H_a)^{-1} \Big[ (I + \tfrac12 S H_a)
\begin{pmatrix} \mathrm{d}\xi_a \\ \mathrm{d}v_a \end{pmatrix}
+ S \, \partial_\theta \nabla\phi_s \, \mathrm{d}\theta - S \begin{pmatrix} \mathrm{d}c \\ \mathrm{d}b \end{pmatrix} \Big],
```

where ``\partial_\theta \nabla \phi_s = (\partial_\theta \partial_v \phi_s, \partial_\theta
\partial_\xi \phi_s)`` and the shift and the boost solve the ``2 \times 2`` system
``(I + T) (\mathrm{d}c, \mathrm{d}b)^\top = r`` with
``T = \tfrac12 \sum_a \alpha_a H_a (I - \tfrac12 S H_a)^{-1} S`` and the right-hand side ``r``
collecting the parameter terms and the midpoint terms of all markers. A fit rejects every
``\theta`` for which the fixed-point iteration needs more than 50 iterations
(`iteration_limit`), which keeps its contraction rate below about ``0.55`` and the step equations
uniquely solvable with a margin.

The tests run every statement of the proposition on seven markers with two random stages in
``d = 1`` and ``d = 2`` (`test/projection/stages.jl`): the velocity form agrees with the implicit
midpoint rule for ``H^N_s`` ([`nonseparable_hamiltonian`](@ref),
[`hamiltonian_one_step`](@ref)) to ``4.4 \cdot 10^{-16}``; two stages have the symplecticity defect
``1.1 \cdot 10^{-15}`` or less and ``|\Delta P| \le 4.4 \cdot 10^{-16}``; the Jacobian
determinant ([`nonseparable_jacobian_determinant`](@ref)) holds to ``4.4 \cdot 10^{-15}`` and the
divergence ([`nonseparable_divergence`](@ref)) to ``8.9 \cdot 10^{-16}``. The cases that must
fail: the trapezoidal rule keeps ``P`` and has the defect ``7.5 \cdot 10^{-4}`` (``d = 1``) and
``3.6 \cdot 10^{-4}`` (``d = 2``); without the centroid shift the stage is symplectic and
``|\Delta P| = 0.29`` and ``1.7``; the first-order determinant misses by at least ``0.315``, the
determinant with ``(I - \tfrac12 \mathbb{J}K)^{-1}`` by at least ``0.475``.

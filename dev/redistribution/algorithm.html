Algorithm ​

The method SplineRedistribution runs the whole redistribution of 1D-1V markers, through remap:

  1. Project the markers onto a tensor-product B-spline basis, periodic in and clamped in , by the positive lumped projection (lumped_spline_projection).

  2. Relax the points by a few damped steps of repulsive gradient descent to targets (declustering_relaxation).

  3. Weigh the targets by the weighted KKT projection,  , with local volumes from the number density of the targets, so that   conserves the discrete mass and momentum (kkt_redistribution_weights).

  4. Solve the linear system   , with  , for the parameters of a pairwise spline Hamiltonian (solve_redistribution_parameters), and advance by one step of the implicit midpoint or the symplectic Euler method, or by a few substeps (forward_redistribution_step, redistribution_substeps). This is the canonical map .

  5. Assign the weights to the end point of the step, and the velocities with the momenta of the end point: the gauge step. By the factorisation lemma, every positive split of the momenta keeps .

The positive projection, the Hamiltonians and the parameter solver give each step in full.

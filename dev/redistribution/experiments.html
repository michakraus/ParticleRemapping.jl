Numerical experiments ​

The standard experiments with the spline-projection redistribution (:spline of standard_method). The comparison sets the results beside those of the other constructions. The construction computes everything from the markers, so the two density variants give the same result. Its own example is on the page the verification set-up, and a lattice with an elliptic hole on the page hole filling.

The full sizes ​

At 400 and at 900 markers the construction stops in the KKT solve, as for the 900 markers of K2: the relaxation sums the repulsion over all pairs, its default step fits 16 markers, and the relaxed targets leave the velocity box of the spline basis.

julia
using ParticleRemapping, CairoMakie
set_theme!(remap_theme())
(checkerboard = standard_checkerboard(:spline).error,
    long_run = standard_long_time(:spline).error)
(checkerboard = "ArgumentError: matrix contains Infs or NaNs", long_run = "ArgumentError: matrix contains Infs or NaNs")

With the relaxation step scaled to  , the 400 markers of the checkerboard reach the end of the remap after 316 s, with the scaled distance   of the end point from the targets: the symplectic step misses its targets, as in K3. The 900 markers of the long run did not finish within 20 minutes.

The pairwise Hamiltonian couples the markers, so the step can move markers into the empty quadrants. It reaches its targets only for the 16 markers of the long run, the size and the kind of distribution of the verification set-up; for every other set the distance from the targets grows with , and the end point is not the step that the parameters describe. The total weight is kept to round-off in every run, the total momentum where the Newton solve of the step converges.

julia
small = standard_checkerboard(:spline; N = 16)
(quadrants = small.quadrants_after, target_error = small.result.info.target_error,
    report = small.report)
(quadrants = (7, 0, 7, 2), target_error = 0.6611457283453605, report = (weight = -2.220446049250313e-16, momentum = 2.3869795029440866e-15, kinetic_energy = 0.08833079649354089))
julia
plot_remap(small.before, small.after;
    targets = (small.result.info.targets_x .- 1, small.result.info.targets_v),
    titles = ("(a) 16 checkerboard markers", "(b) after the redistribution"))

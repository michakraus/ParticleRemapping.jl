The algorithm runs the steps of the construction in this order:

  3. Lift the positions to and compute and .

  5. Apply with the optimal .

remap with a KickDriftRemap runs these steps for 1D-1V Markers; NonSeparableRemap replaces the kicks and drifts by non-separable stages, and the option charge adds the charge-conserving reweighting. The example of the overview runs the algorithm on a lattice of   markers with the target weights . The example below runs it on the same markers with the target weights from the lumped projection (the default) on   hats, coarser than the markers, and a fit stopped after 10 iterations. On hats finer than the marker spacing the lumped projection vanishes between the markers, and remap refuses the zero target weights.

julia
using ParticleRemapping, CairoMakie, Random
Random.seed!(20261119)
small = hole_filling_problem(; hole_x = π / 2, nL = 24, hole_a = 0.9, hole_b = 0.8)
result = remap(KickDriftRemap(; projection = HatProjection(12, 13), maxit = 10), small.markers)
(; objective_ratio = result.info.objective_ratio, iterations = result.info.iterations,
    momentum_change = result.info.momentum_change, weight_change = result.info.weight_change)
(objective_ratio = 0.6358669585690309, iterations = 10, momentum_change = 0.0, weight_change = 3.055943492699168e-16)
julia
plot_remap(small.markers, result.markers;
    titles = ("(a) before", "(b) after the remap, lumped projection"), period = (0, 2π))

Verification: gauge rezoning and canonical transport ​

Every claim and every number of the chapter Gauge rezoning and canonical transport is checked by a test file in test/remapping/, and each check runs with a control that must fail; the control asserts the margin by which it fails. The scripts in scripts/remapping/ print the numbers of the tables and check them again. Run a test file with

julia --startup-file=no --project=test test/remapping/<file>.jl

and a script with

julia --startup-file=no --project=scripts scripts/remapping/<file>.jl

Counts against the source note ​

The worked examples of the source note give 21 checks with their controls, the three verifications of the symplecticity condition, the gauge rezoning and the matching 7, 10 and 9; the tests port all 47, with the numbers of the tables at the digits they are quoted with.

The necessity in the factorisation lemma, the second-order proposition of the gauge rezoning, the limit of equidistribution and the proposition on admissible generators in general rest on the proofs given in the chapter; the tests check special cases only.

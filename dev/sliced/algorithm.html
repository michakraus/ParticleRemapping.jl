Per-axis version for   ​

For   positions and velocities the pairs ,  , are canonically independent. remap applies steps 1–4 to each axis (velocity slices by , position slices by ), computes one set of multipliers with the product kernel in all coordinates, and squeezes every axis with these . per_axis_sliced_lift is the lift of each axis with given slices. Each lift sees the two-dimensional marginal only: a hole in the correlation of two axes is not addressed (higher dimensions).

Each lift assembles the dense   Hessian, non-trivial entries plus the rank-one term , and solves with a dense LU factorisation, .

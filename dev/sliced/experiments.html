Numerical experiments ​

The standard experiments with the sliced redistribution (:sliced of standard_method). The comparison sets the results beside those of the other constructions. The construction computes everything from the markers, so the two density variants give the same result. The structural checks are on the page structural suites, the lifts alone on the checkerboard on the page hole filling, and the variants of the parameters of the long run on the page long-time run: variants and repeated remaps.

The checkerboard ​

The full remap, with the lifts, the squeeze and the gauge step, on   with  ,   and the kernel widths   :

weight in  empty cells of 64relative std
before200020000321.022
after13467128710.20520.606

The lifts couple the markers through their slices and move markers into the empty quadrants (hole filling). The squeeze then sets the weights from a kernel estimate of the density of the input, which is small in and : the markers there carry the weight together, against for the positive density and zero for the represented one. The squeeze moves some positions by up to the half width of the box (K6). The total weight and the total momentum are kept to round-off.

julia
using ParticleRemapping, CairoMakie
set_theme!(remap_theme())
board = standard_checkerboard(:sliced)
(quadrants = board.quadrants_after, hole_weight = board.hole_weight,
    stats = board.stats_after[(:empty, :rstd)], max_shift = board.max_shift,
    report = board.report)
(quadrants = (134, 67, 128, 71), hole_weight = 0.2049254352286328, stats = (empty = 2, rstd = 0.6064583436840942), max_shift = 0.9966440504848249, report = (weight = -1.1102230246251565e-16, momentum = 0.0, kinetic_energy = 0.007242245284279608))
julia
plot_remap(board.before, board.after; period = (-1, 1),
    titles = ("(a) before", "(b) after the sliced remap"))

The lifts ​

ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.sliced_quantile_potential Function
julia
sliced_quantile_potential(u, bins, taus; β_mean = 0.60, β_intra = 0.72, ε = 0.4)

The strictly convex potential of the sliced quantile lift,

julia
S(u) = (1/2N) (Σ_c u_c)² + (1 − β_mean)/2 Σ_b n_b (ū_b − ū)² + (1 − β_intra)/2 Σ_b Σ_{a∈b} (u_a − ū_b)²
     + Σ_b β_intra / (2 n_b κ_b) Σ_{r,s} |τ_r − τ_s| √((u_{a_r} − u_{a_s})² + ε²),

with ū the mean of all u, ū_b the mean over slice b (the indices bins[b], n_b of them, in the order of the targets taus[b]) and κ_b the attenuation factor of the slice. Its gradient is the map of split_quantile_ot_lift.

ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.sliced_quantile_hessian Function
julia
sliced_quantile_hessian(u, bins, taus; β_mean = 0.60, β_intra = 0.72, ε = 0.4)

The Hessian ∇²S(u) of the sliced_quantile_potential: the Jacobian of the map u ↦ ∇S(u) of split_quantile_ot_lift, a symmetric matrix with ∇²S ⪰ (1 − max(β_mean, β_intra)) I and ∇²S 1 = 1.

ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.split_quantile_ot_lift Function
julia
split_quantile_ot_lift(u, v, bins, taus; anchors = nothing, β_mean = 0.60, β_intra = 0.72,
                       ε = 0.4, with_cotangent = true) -> (ũ, ṽ)

The anchored cotangent lift of the gradient map ũ = ∇S(u) of the sliced_quantile_potential S:

julia
ũ_a = β_mean ū + (β_intra − β_mean) ū_b + (1 − β_intra) u_a
      + β_intra / (n_b κ_b) Σ_{s} |τ_r − τ_s| (u_a − u_{a_s}) / √((u_a − u_{a_s})² + ε²),
ṽ   = v_anc + (∇²S(u))⁻¹ (v − v_anc),

for marker a = a_r of slice b. Within each slice the ordered markers relax towards the targets taus[b]; the slice means relax towards the mean by the factor β_mean, the deviations within a slice by β_intra. The Hessian ∇²S is symmetric with ∇²S ⪰ (1 − max(β_mean, β_intra)) I and ∇²S 1 = 1, so the lift is a symplectic diffeomorphism of (u, v) that keeps Σ u and Σ v. The anchors v_anc (zero without anchors) are constants.

(u, v) is (q, p) for the lift in the positions and (p, q) for the dual_quantile_ot_lift. With with_cotangent = false the conjugate variable is returned unchanged: the map is then not symplectic (a control).

ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.dual_quantile_ot_lift Function
julia
dual_quantile_ot_lift(q, p, bins, taus; anchors = nothing, kwargs...) -> (q̂, p̂)

The dual lift: split_quantile_ot_lift with the roles of positions and momenta exchanged, p̂ = ∇S(p), q̂ = q_anc + (∇²S(p))⁻¹ (q − q_anc). It equalises the momenta within each slice and is symplectic for the same reasons, since (q, p) ↦ (p, −q) is.

ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.per_axis_sliced_lift Function
julia
per_axis_sliced_lift(x, p, bins, taus; kwargs...) -> (x̃, p̃)

The split_quantile_ot_lift of each axis ℓ of d × N positions and momenta, (x̃[ℓ, :], p̃[ℓ, :]) = Ψ_ℓ(x[ℓ, :], p[ℓ, :]), with the slices bins[ℓ] and the targets taus (the same for all axes). The pairs (x_ℓ, p_ℓ) are canonically conjugate and the lifts of different axes act on different pairs, so the product is symplectic for Σ_ℓ dx_ℓ ∧ dp_ℓ and keeps each component of Σ p. The keyword arguments go to every lift.

The weight squeeze and the weights ​

ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.canonical_weight_squeeze Function
julia
canonical_weight_squeeze(q, p, μ, q_anc) -> (q̄, p̄)

The momentum-conserving canonical weight squeeze with positive slot multipliers μ, Σ_a μ_a = N:

julia
p̄_a = μ_a (p_a + (1/N) Σ_c (1 − μ_c) p_c),
q̄_a = q_anc,a + ((q_a − q_anc,a) − (1 − μ_a) (1/N) Σ_c (q_c − q_anc,c)) / μ_a .

It is the linear cotangent lift p̄ = M p, q̄ − q_anc = M⁻ᵀ (q − q_anc) of M = diag(μ) (I + (1/N) 1 (1 − μ)ᵀ), whose inverse transpose the Sherman–Morrison formula gives in closed form because Σ μ = N. It keeps Σ dq ∧ dp and Σ p. The anchors q_anc are constants. Throws an ArgumentError unless Σ μ = N to round-off.

ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.kernel_density_ratio Function
julia
kernel_density_ratio(xe, ve, x, v, w; hx = 0.45, hv = 0.35, Lx = π)

The ratio r_a = Σ_c w_c K(xe_a − x_c, ve_a − v_c) / Σ_c K(xe_a − xe_c, ve_a − ve_c) at the evaluation points (xe, ve): a kernel estimate of the density f of the weighted markers (x, v, w) divided by a kernel estimate of the density of the evaluation points themselves. It is the weight that a marker at (xe_a, ve_a) carries when the evaluation points sample f. The kernel is Gaussian with widths hx, hv and periodic in each position component with period 2Lx. Positions and velocities are vectors (1D-1V) or d × N matrices.

ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.squeeze_multipliers Function
julia
squeeze_multipliers(q̂, p̂, q_anc, x, v, w; m, w_ref = sum(w) / length(w), hx = 0.45,
                    hv = 0.35, ratio_floor = 0.08, iterations = 3, Lx = π)

The slot multipliers μ (Σ μ = N) of the canonical_weight_squeeze from the density ratio of the input markers (x, v, w), by a predictor–corrector loop. Start from the points (q̂, p̂ / (m w_ref)) and repeat iterations times:

  1. μ_a ∝ r_a + ratio_floor · w_ref with r the kernel_density_ratio at the current points, normalised to Σ μ = N;

  2. squeeze (q̂, p̂) with these μ (along each axis for d × N arrays) and evaluate at the squeezed points (q̄, p̄ / (m w_ref μ)).

The result depends on the state, so a squeeze with it is canonical for frozen μ only.

The composed map ​

ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.SlicedLiftPlan Type
julia
SlicedLiftPlan

The data of the two lifts Ψ_q and Ψ_p of a sliced redistribution, read from one input state and frozen: the velocity slices bins_v with their position targets taus_x and momentum anchors p_anc, the position slices bins_x with their momentum targets taus_p and position anchors q_anc, the slot velocity slot_v of each marker (the velocity target of its rank within its position slice), and the parameters β_mean, β_intra, ε of each lift. For a frozen plan, apply_sliced_lift is a smooth symplectic map of (q, p). Build it with sliced_lift_plan.

ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.sliced_lift_plan Function
julia
sliced_lift_plan(x, v, p; m, w_ref, Lx, Vmax, B_v, B_x, β_mean_q = 0.60, β_intra_q = 0.72,
                 ε_q = 0.4, β_mean_p = 0.60, β_intra_p = 0.72, ε_p) -> SlicedLiftPlan

The plan of the sliced lifts for markers with positions x, velocities v and momenta p:

  1. B_v velocity slices of n_v = N / B_v markers, each ordered by x; the position targets are the uniform_quantile_targets of [−Lx, Lx] with n_v points; the momentum anchor of a marker is m w_ref times the centre of its velocity slice in [−Vmax, Vmax];

  2. the lift Ψ_q with these data, run once to find q̃, p̃;

  3. B_x position slices of n_x = N / B_x markers by q̃, each ordered by p̃; the position anchor is the centre of the slice in [−Lx, Lx]; the momentum targets are m w_ref times the n_x uniform quantile targets of [−Vmax, Vmax].

The slices and anchors are piecewise constant functions of the state: the composed map is smooth and symplectic where they do not change, and they change where two markers swap their order.

ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.apply_sliced_lift Function
julia
apply_sliced_lift(plan::SlicedLiftPlan, q, p; with_cotangent = true) -> (; q̃, p̃, q̂, p̂)

The two lifts of a frozen plan: (q̃, p̃) = Ψ_q(q, p) equalises the positions within each velocity slice (split_quantile_ot_lift), (q̂, p̂) = Ψ_p(q̃, p̃) the momenta within each position slice (dual_quantile_ot_lift). Both keep Σ dq ∧ dp and Σ p. with_cotangent = false drops the cotangent parts (a control).

ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.SlicedQuantileRemap Type
julia
SlicedQuantileRemap(; Lx = π, Vmax = 2.25, B_v = 15, B_x = 90, β_mean_q = 0.75,
                    β_intra_q = 0.78, ε_q_spacings = 2.0, β_mean_p = 0.78, β_intra_p = 0.80,
                    ε_p_spacings = 2.0, hx = 0.45, hv = 0.35, ratio_floor = 0.08,
                    iterations = 3)

The sliced symplectic redistribution R = F ∘ T_μ ∘ Ψ_p ∘ Ψ_q on the box [−Lx, Lx) × [−Vmax, Vmax] (periodic in x), with the parameters of the long-time experiment:

  • Ψ_q, Ψ_p: the lifts of sliced_lift_plan with B_v velocity and B_x position slices; the mollifier widths are ε_q_spacings target spacings 2Lx / n_v and ε_p_spacings spacings 2Lp / n_x, with Lp the largest momentum target;

  • T_μ: the canonical_weight_squeeze with the squeeze_multipliers (kernel widths hx, hv, floor ratio_floor, iterations predictor–corrector passes);

  • F: the weights w̄ = w_ref μ and velocities v̄ = p̄ / (m w̄).

N must be a multiple of B_v and B_x. For 1D-1V markers the map keeps Σ w, Σ p and, for the frozen plan and multipliers, Σ dx ∧ dp. For d × N markers stages 1–3 act on each axis with one common μ (see remap).

ParticleRemapping.remap Method
julia
remap(method::SlicedQuantileRemap, mk::Markers) -> RemapResult

Redistribute the markers by the sliced map R = F ∘ T_μ ∘ Ψ_p ∘ Ψ_q (see SlicedQuantileRemap). The info holds the plan (a SlicedLiftPlan, one per axis for d × N markers), the intermediate states q̃, p̃, q̂, p̂, the squeezed state q̄, p̄, the multipliers μ and the reference weight w_ref = Σ w / N. The positions are not wrapped into [−Lx, Lx).

ParticleRemapping.remap Method
julia
remap(method::SlicedQuantileRemap, mk::Markers{T, <:AbstractMatrix}) -> RemapResult

The sliced redistribution of d × N markers: for each axis ℓ the lifts Ψ_q, Ψ_p of the pair (x_ℓ, p_ℓ), sliced by v_ℓ and by q̃_ℓ (one SlicedLiftPlan per axis); then one set of multipliers μ from the 2d-dimensional kernel_density_ratio, the canonical_weight_squeeze of each axis with this μ, and w̄ = w_ref μ, v̄ = p̄ / (m w̄). Each lift sees the two-dimensional marginal (x_ℓ, v_ℓ) only: correlations between different axes are not equalised. info holds the vector of plans and the intermediate d × N states.

The frames of an animation of the sliced remap of before that gave result, along a path of canonical maps: frames_per_stage frames of Ψ_q and of Ψ_p with the relaxation parameters β_mean, β_intra of the frozen plan scaled by s ∈ (0, 1] (the identity at s = 0); of the squeeze T_μ with the multipliers (1 − s) + s μ, which keep Σ μ = N; all at the old weights, v = p / (m w). ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.factorisation_pullback_experiment Function
julia
factorisation_pullback_experiment(; seed = 101, N = 8, m = 1.35)

The factorisation on the full state space (x, v, w) (suite 1). The state z = (x, v, w) of N random markers is mapped to the canonical output (q̄, p̄ = m w̄ v̄) by three maps, and the pull-back of Σ dq̄ ∧ dp̄ is compared with that of Σ dq ∧ dp (the presymplectic form on (x, v, w)):

  • full: a split_quantile_ot_lift of (q, p) = (x, m w v) (slices 1:4, 5:8, targets ±0.5, ±1.5, β_mean = 0.5, β_intra = 0.7), new weights w̄ ∝ w (1 + 0.3 cos q̄ e^{−v²/4}) with Σ w̄ = Σ w, and v̄ = p̄ / (m w̄);

  • no_recoil: the same with v̄ = p̄ / (m w) (a control that must fail);

  • leaf: q̄ = q, p̄ = p − w sin q, a map that reads w at fixed (q, p): canonical on each leaf w = const, not across leaves (a control that must fail across dw).

Returns the largest entries err_full, err_no_recoil, err_on_leaf (variations of x, v only) and err_across_dw (all variations).

ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.pseudo_time_flow_experiment Function
julia
pseudo_time_flow_experiment(; seed = 202, N = 6, m = 1.1, Δτ = 0.12, iterations = 25,
                            rate = 0.25)

The pseudo-time flow with a weight rate (suite 2). One implicit-midpoint step Δτ of the generator G(q, p) = (1/4) Σ_{a,b} exp(−(q_a − q_b)²/2) (p_a − p_b)² (fixed-point iteration, iterations sweeps) moves (q, p); the weights change as w̄_a = w_a exp(Δτ · rate · cos q̄_a). ParticleRemapping.SlicedRedistribution.autonomous_map_experiment Function

autonomous_map_experiment(; seed = 303, N = 16, m = 1.25, Lx = π, Vmax = 2.0, B_v = 4,
                          B_x = 4, N2 = 12)

The autonomous four-stage map in d = 1 and d = 2 with its controls (suite 3).

julia
repulsion_wall_probe(; seed = 42, N = 400, passes = 12, α = 0.12, σ = 0.35, k_w = 0.4)

The cotangent lift of Φ(Q) = Q − α ∇U(Q) with a Gaussian pair potential of width σ and the quartic wall k_w Q⁴/4 (in units of the action u₀ = √(m/N)), applied in six rotated frames per pass to the checkerboard markers of the probe (weights ∝ 1 + 0.5 cos(πx) e^{−v²}). The Jacobian DΦ = I − α ∇²U is symmetric; the wall adds −3 α k_w Q_a² to its diagonal. Returns the first sub-step (pass, frame) and the eigenvalue at which DΦ stops being positive definite (so that Φ folds), and the sub-step at which DΦ stops being finite (nothing if never).

julia
checkerboard_reweighting_control(; h = 0.25, kwargs...)

The checkerboard of checkerboard_experiment after its sliced lifts, reweighted at fixed momenta: w̄ ∝ r with r the kernel_density_ratio of the input markers at the lifted points (kernel width h in x and v, periodic on [−1, 1)), Σ w̄ = Σ w, and v̄ = p̂ / (m w̄).
